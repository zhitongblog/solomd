• VS Code Shortcut Preset: One-click preset mapping Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+P to Command Palette and Cmd/Ctrl+B to Bold.
• Multi-Device Sync Polish: Auto-sync on network reconnect, synced file-tree order & sort mode, and per-device UUID sync journal.
