SoloMD 5.0 — a calmer place to write.

• A redesigned window: a quiet header with Live / Source / Split / Preview one tap apart, a sidebar for your folders and notes, pill-shaped tabs, and a small word-count pill instead of a status bar.
• iPhone: a notes home screen, search and a new-note button at the bottom, and a full-screen editor.
• iPad: in portrait the sidebar slides over the page instead of squeezing it.
• A new find bar: it stays out of the way, keeps your place, and supports match case, whole word and regular expressions.
• Select text for a formatting bubble; on iPhone and iPad a keyboard bar adds headings, lists, links and code.
• Fold headings from the gutter to see the shape of a long document.
• Menus and shortcuts reorganized; every shortcut can be rebound, and shortcuts show ⌘ on iPad.
• Faster first paint: math and code highlighting load after the page appears.
• Chinese proofreading no longer flags file names like 文档.md or an ellipsis as punctuation mistakes.

Thanks to 张工 for weeks of careful testing that shaped this release.
