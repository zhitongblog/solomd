SoloMD is a calm, fast Markdown editor for the Mac. Open the app and you are writing — no account, no sign-up, no subscription.

Your notes are plain .md and .txt files in folders you choose. Open a folder you already use and edit the files right where they are. Any editor on any platform can read them, today and in ten years.

WRITING FIRST

- Live editing: Markdown marks step aside once the cursor leaves the line, so you see the document, not the syntax.
- Real typography: big headings, true bold, monospaced code, comfortable line length.
- Four views one click apart: live, source, split with synced scrolling, and preview.
- Focus mode dims everything but the paragraph you are in; typewriter mode keeps your line at eye level.
- A formatting bubble appears over selected text.
- Find and replace with match case, whole word and regular expressions.
- Light and dark themes that follow your Mac.

A MAC APP

- A quiet window: sidebar with folders and notes, tabs, and panels that stay out of the way until you call them.
- Native menus with a shortcut for nearly everything — and you can rebind any of them.
- Command palette and quick switcher.
- Double-click any .md or .txt file to open it. Multiple windows.
- Small download, fast launch, native on Apple Silicon and Intel.

MARKDOWN THAT GOES FURTHER

- KaTeX math, inline and display.
- Mermaid flowcharts, sequence diagrams and Gantt charts.
- Tables with a visual table editor, task lists, footnotes, front matter, highlights.
- Syntax highlighting for 13+ languages.
- Fold headings to see the shape of a long document.
- Outline, backlinks for [[wiki links]], and search across the whole folder.

FOR CHINESE WRITERS

- Simplified ↔ Traditional conversion, pinyin, and CJK-aware word counts.
- Chinese proofreading for half-width punctuation and common slips.
- Opens UTF-8, UTF-16, GBK and Big5 files correctly.

EXPORT

- PDF with selectable text, math and diagrams rendered.
- Word (.docx) with tables kept, optionally from your own template.
- HTML, or copy as HTML, plain text or Markdown.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

SoloMD collects no data. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Your notes stay in files you own.
